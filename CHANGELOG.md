## [0.1.0] - 2026-10-05

### Added

- First release. The skill loads when an agent writes, rewrites or translates into English prose for people: docs, README files, changelogs and release notes, pull request descriptions, articles, web pages, reports, emails and posts. It also loads when the text is one step of a larger task or goes to a subagent.
- Formatting by genre without bold, emoji or dividers, and with headings where readers need them rather than over every two sentences.
- A rewrite for each job an em dash usually does, so the sentence stays whole instead of turning into a semicolon, a staged reveal or a fragment.
- Sentence length that follows the thought, without a run of short sentences of the same length.
- No filler, no flags of importance in place of a reason, no contrasts with claims nobody made, and no endings with a moral or unasked advice.
- No made-up figures, studies, events or promises, and no claim made bigger than the material allows.
- A project's own format, such as a Keep a Changelog heading or a PR template, wins over the layout rules.
- A final check the agent runs on the written file before handing it over.
